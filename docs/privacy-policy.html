Dṛṣṭi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Dṛṣṭi is a personal decision companion for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how Dṛṣṭi handles information when you use the app.

1. Information stored on your device

Dṛṣṭi is designed to keep your personal information on your device. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Birth and profile details you enter, such as name, date of birth, time of birth and birth location.
  • Places and locations saved in the app.
  • Chat conversations and messages.
  • Decisions, decision analyses and outcome information you choose to record.
  • Locally calculated or cached astronomical and Jyotish information.
  • Application settings and local diagnostic metadata.

This information is stored locally by the app and is not uploaded to a Dṛṣṭi-operated account or central Dṛṣṭi server. The developer of Dṛṣṭi does not have technical access to the data stored on your device.

2. AI providers

Dṛṣṭi can operate in an offline/mock mode or use a live AI provider selected and configured by you. When live AI is enabled, Dṛṣṭi sends the information needed for the requested AI operation to your selected provider.

This may include your current message and relevant information derived from your locally stored data, such as indicators used for the requested analysis. Dṛṣṭi does not intentionally send your entire local database to an AI provider for every request.

The selected AI provider processes information according to its own privacy policy, terms and data-handling practices. You are responsible for reviewing the provider's policies before enabling a provider.

3. Your AI API keys

If you configure an AI provider API key, Dṛṣṭi stores that credential using encrypted on-device storage techniques where supported by your device. API keys are not intentionally included in Dṛṣṭi's user data exports or sent to Dṛṣṭi-operated servers.

To simplify setup, Dṛṣṭi may optionally read your clipboard specifically to detect valid API key formats (e.g., keys starting with AIza). This detection happens entirely on your device; clipboard contents are never stored or transmitted by Dṛṣṭi.

You should treat your API key as sensitive information and should not share it with other people.

4. Network access

Dṛṣṭi uses network access only when a feature requires it, such as communicating with a live AI provider or performing an explicitly supported import/export operation. The app can be used in offline/mock mode without a live AI provider.

5. Diagnostics and request metadata

Dṛṣṭi may keep local metadata about AI requests, such as request type, model, prompt version, timestamps, latency and cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) used for provenance or diagnostics. These hashes allow for request tracking without storing the actual text content of your questions or the AI's responses in the diagnostic log.

6. Backups and device transfer

Android backup and device-transfer behavior is controlled by the app's backup configuration and Android's platform behavior. Dṛṣṭi explicitly excludes its encrypted API-key storage from cloud backup and device transfer. API keys are therefore not intended to be copied through Dṛṣṭi's Android backup configuration.

Dṛṣṭi's own user-level backup/export is intentionally limited to supported application data and does not include encrypted API credentials or regenerable internal calculation data.

7. Data deletion

You can delete supported profiles, conversations and decision records from within Dṛṣṭi. You can also clear configured API credentials from the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data subject to Android's backup and restore behavior.

Information already sent to a third-party AI provider is subject to that provider's retention and deletion policies. Dṛṣṭi cannot delete information that has already been processed or retained by a third-party provider.

8. No sale of personal information

Dṛṣṭi does not sell your personal information. Dṛṣṭi does not operate a service that sells user profiles, birth information or chat histories to advertisers or other third parties.

9. Children

Dṛṣṭi is not designed specifically for children. Please do not provide information belonging to a child unless you have the appropriate authority and the use is lawful.

10. Security

Dṛṣṭi uses Android platform security features and encrypted local storage for API credentials. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Dṛṣṭi's data handling or functionality changes. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the effective date shown above.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the developer contact information provided on Dṛṣṭi's Google Play listing.

13. Third-party AI services

Dṛṣṭi may support multiple AI providers. The exact providers available can change as the app evolves. When you choose a provider, your use of that provider is also governed by the provider's own terms and privacy policy.
